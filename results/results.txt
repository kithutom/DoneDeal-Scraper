8. FORECAST: WILL THIS GOLF BE WORTH MORE OR LESS THAN OTHERS OF ITS AGE?
==============================================================================
The forecast tree uses only what is known about the car years ahead:
  Mileage (km), Km per year, Age (years), Previous owners, Engine size (L), Trim level, Fuel, Transmission, Body type
  (left out, because they describe the ad: Seller type, Warranty, NCT, County)
Depth 5; test accuracy 72.7%; mean over 30 splits 69.5% (all attributes: 69.5%)

The car is moved forward: age + N years, mileage + N x its km per year so far.
The tree, trained on Golfs that are that age today, then classifies the future car.
Typical price = todays price of Golfs of that age, group and trim, in todays euro.
